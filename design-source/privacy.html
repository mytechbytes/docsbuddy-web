Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocsBuddy ("we", "us", the "app") collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use our mobile application and website at docsbuddy.mytechbytes.in. By using DocsBuddy you agree to the practices described here.

DocsBuddy is a personal asset and reminder tracker. We collect only what is needed to run the service, never sell your data, and never use your documents for advertising.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — your name and email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Asset & reminder data — the assets you add (vehicles, appliances, electronics, etc.), their details, and the due dates you track (insurance, AMC, pollution, warranty, registration, fitness, tax).
  • Documents you upload — invoices, warranty cards, insurance policies, photos and similar files you choose to attach to an asset.
  • Family / sharing data — the family group you create or join and the members you invite.
  • Device & push tokens — a push-notification token and basic device/platform information so we can deliver reminders.
  • Usage & diagnostic data — limited technical logs (e.g. crashes, errors) used to keep the app reliable.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service — storing your assets, documents and reminders, and syncing them across your devices and family members.
  • To send you renewal reminders and notifications via push, email or WhatsApp according to your settings.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account and data secure.
  • To diagnose problems, improve performance and develop new features.
  • To respond to your support requests.

3. Service providers we use

We rely on trusted third-party platforms to operate DocsBuddy. Your data may be processed by:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage for your account, asset data and documents.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivery of push notifications and reminders to your device.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We share only what is necessary for them to perform these functions.

4. Legal basis & consent

We process your information to perform our contract with you (providing the app), with your consent (e.g. notification channels and document uploads), and to pursue our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and reliable. You can withdraw consent at any time by changing your settings or deleting your account.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only: (a) with the service providers listed above; (b) with the family members you choose to invite, who can view and update shared assets; and (c) where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete an asset or document it is removed from active storage, and when you delete your account your personal data is deleted within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal obligations.

7. Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by our providers with access controls and row-level security so that only you and your family can read your records. Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure keystore. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

8. Your rights

  • Access & portability — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update inaccurate information directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete individual assets, documents, or your whole account.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off notification channels or revoke document access at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children's privacy

DocsBuddy is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. Where this happens, we rely on our providers' safeguards to protect your data in line with applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, communicated in the app.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@docsbuddy.mytechbytes.in.
